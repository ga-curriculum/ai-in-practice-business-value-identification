## About

By the end of this session, you will be equipped to:

- **Analyze real-world AI applications and identify success factors.** You'll explore case studies across industries to understand what makes AI initiatives successful.
- **Apply a structured framework to evaluate AI business opportunities.** You'll engage in a hands-on activity to assess the potential value of AI use cases.
- **Identify and mitigate common challenges and risks in AI implementation.** Using real-world failure cases, you will diagnose key risks and develop strategies to address them.
